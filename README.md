# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is a *science fiction horror* series set in the 1980s in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana. It follows a group of friends who uncover secret experiments and a mysterious world called the ***Upside Down***.

---

## Why I Like It
- Exciting and suspenseful story
- Characters who feel like real friends
- Nostalgic 1980s setting and music
- Great mix of mystery, friendship, and adventure
